Red Panda Zoo

Naturalistic Habitats for Viewing, Educational Programs and Places to Dine

The Red Panda Zoo has over 100 pandas. Visitors can view the pandas eating, sleeping and climbing in the many living spaces.

The Zoo also offers daily educational programs focused on Red Pandas. Visitors will learn about red pandas' natural habitats, eating habits, and solitary lifestyles.

The Zoo has two dining options: the Bamboo Cafe and the Panda Coffee Shop.

A gift shop offers a variety of panda-themed items for all ages.

The Zoo is surrounded by beautifully landscaped grounds and picnic areas.

There are paths through forested areas containing five different species of bamboo.

Join us to learn more about these amazing pandas!
