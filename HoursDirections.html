Red Panda Zoo

Hours and Directions

The Zoo is open Tuesday-Sunday, 10-5 pm. The Zoo is closed on Thanksgiving, Dec. 25, Dec. 31, and January 1.

The Zoo is located on 10 Hillside Road, Westport, Pennsylvania.

Parking is free in the designated parking lots.
