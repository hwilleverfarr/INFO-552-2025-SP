Red Panda Zoo

About

The Red Panda Zoo was founded in 2001 with the generous funds of the Wildlife Federation. It began as a small habitat for pandas on land donated by a retired farmer in Westport, Pennsylvania. The Wildlife Federation provided more funding to increase the size of the living areas for the pandas. Now, the Zoo cares for 50 pandas.

In 2010, the Zoo added dining facilties and a gift shop.

In 2018, surrounding, unused land was landscaped with different bamboo species and paths were added later for visitors to explore these amazing plants.
